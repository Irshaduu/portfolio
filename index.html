WorkshopOS NEW!

One system for a premium car workshop that ran on paper job cards, WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

Built for Formula D in Pullara, Kerala. I mapped how the workshop works on the floor, then built one Django and PostgreSQL system to replace all three: job cards, inventory, supplier ledgers, payments, payroll, estimates, warranty claims and three-tier role-based access.

  • Stock is costed by weighted average, replayed in date order. A back-dated supplier bill re-prices only the draws after it, and negative stock still works.
  • 21 rule modules, each answering one question, such as How much do we hand the rent collector today? No figure can be worked out two different ways.
  • 72 all-or-nothing transaction blocks guard every money movement, with a Change History audit trail.
  • Per-account and per-IP lockout, device-aware sessions with remote revoke, Web Push, and photo uploads signed with AWS SigV4 using only the standard library.

Word Hunt NEW!

A real-time multiplayer word game for voice calls with friends.

Up to ten players. One secretly picks a word from a chaotic board of 71, and everyone else hunts it while a 100-second time bank drains. Room codes are three letters, so you can say them out loud.

  • A six-state, server-authoritative state machine on Django Channels.
  • A dropped connection marks you disconnected, not removed, so a network blip never costs your time bank. Reconnects back off exponentially and resync the full state.
  • Every in-game sound is synthesised with the Web Audio API. No audio files, no Redis, no bill.

Therapist

A hands-free knee rehab coach I built for my own recovery.

After knee surgery, counting 10-second holds and 20 reps through pain was a job of its own. Therapist counts for you, out loud, in English or Malayalam. Press start and put the phone down. I used it every day of my rehabilitation.

  • Three-tier voice: the browser's speech engine, then 215 pre-generated English and Malayalam clips, then a server endpoint. A stall timeout clears the browser's known hung-speech bug.
  • Offline-first: sessions queue in localStorage and sync when the network returns.
  • Screen Wake Lock keeps the display on in the middle of an exercise.
