WorkshopOS NEW!

One system for a premium car workshop that ran on paper job cards, WhatsApp and spreadsheets.

Built for Formula D in Pullara, Kerala, whose owners had tried off-the-shelf workshop software without finding a fit. I mapped how the floor really works, then built one Django and PostgreSQL system for job cards, stock, supplier ledgers, payroll, estimates and warranty claims, with three roles: owner, office and floor.

  • 21 rule modules each own one question, such as How much do we hand the rent collector today? No figure can be worked out two ways.
  • Stock is costed by weighted average, replayed in date order, so a supplier bill keyed weeks late re-prices only what came after it.
  • Every money movement runs in one of 72 all-or-nothing transactions, and every delete, edit and back-dated entry lands in a Change History.
  • Per-account and per-IP lockout, remote session revoke, and photo uploads signed with AWS SigV4 from the standard library, checked against Amazon's test vector.

Word Hunt NEW!

A real-time multiplayer word game for voice calls with friends.

Up to ten players. One secretly picks a word from a board of 71, and everyone else hunts it while a 100-second time bank drains. Room codes are three letters, so you can say them out loud.

  • A six-state, server-authoritative state machine on Django Channels.
  • A dropped connection marks you disconnected, not removed, so a network blip never costs your time bank. Reconnects back off and resync the full state.
  • Every sound is synthesised with the Web Audio API and the channel layer runs in memory: no audio files, no Redis, no bill.

PhysioCoach

A hands-free knee rehab coach I built for my own recovery.

After knee surgery, counting 10-second holds and 20 reps through pain was a job of its own. PhysioCoach counts out loud, in English or Malayalam: press Start and put the phone down. I used it every day of my recovery.

  • Three-tier voice: the browser's speech engine, then 215 pre-generated English and Malayalam clips, then a server endpoint. A stall timeout clears the browser's known hung-speech bug.
  • Offline-first: sessions queue in localStorage and sync when the network returns.
  • Screen Wake Lock keeps the display on mid-exercise.
